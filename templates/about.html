Project Overview

About FarmDirect

Farmer-to-Consumer Direct Agricultural Marketplace

Project Motivation & Objectives

In traditional agricultural supply chains, smallholder farmers often receive less than 25-35% of the end consumer price due to multi-tiered brokers, wholesale cartels, and commission agents. FarmDirect addresses this fundamental economic imbalance by providing an intuitive, transparent digital platform where farmers can sell directly to end-consumers.

Direct Remuneration

Eliminating intermediaries ensures farmers gain maximum profit for their hard work and harvest.

Freshness & Quality

Produce reaches consumer tables within 24-48 hours of harvest rather than sitting in warehouse storages.

Multi-Role Architecture

The system cleanly segments business operations across four essential roles:

Role 1: Farmer

Add/edit products, manage stock quantities, and view orders for their crops.

Role 2: Customer

Browse farm catalog, search, filter, cart management, checkout, and post-delivery reviews.

Role 3: Delivery

View assigned orders, pick up packages from farmers, and update live shipment statuses.

Role 4: Admin

Verify farmers, manage users and products, track marketplace orders, and resolve complaints.
